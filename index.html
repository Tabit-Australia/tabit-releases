Someone walks in.
Your screen changes.

A small motion sensor that watches your doorway, and a Windows app that does what you tell it to.

Download for Windows Join the Discord

Windows 10 or 11 · 64-bit

What it does

Watches the door, not you

A passive infrared sensor sees movement, nothing else. No camera, no microphone, and nothing leaves your desk.

You choose what happens

Switch window, show the desktop, lock the PC, open a program, pause your music. One arrival, one action.

Quiet when you want it

Quiet hours, a pause button on your phone, and an undo for when it fires and you would rather it had not.

Setting it up

  1. Install the appRun the installer. It takes a few seconds and puts Tab It in your Start menu.

  2. Plug your Tab It inAny USB port. The app finds it on its own and walks you through naming it.

  3. Point it at the doorwayGive it about twenty seconds to settle, then walk in front of it.

Before you install

Windows will warn you. You will see “Windows protected your PC — unknown publisher”. Tab It is not code-signed yet, which is a certificate we have not bought rather than anything wrong with the download. Choose More info then Run anyway.
